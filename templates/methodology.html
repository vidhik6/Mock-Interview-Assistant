How your feedback is produced

Methodology

A transparent, practice-first scoring system that highlights what to keep and what to improve next.

01

Relevance

Compares the meaning and vocabulary of your response with the model answer so you stay focused on the question.

02

Completeness

Checks whether the key points in the reference answer are represented in your response.

03

Keyword coverage

Looks for important concepts, tools, outcomes, and role-specific language without rewarding repetition alone.

04

Delivery signals

Reviews answer length and fluency. Behavioral answers also receive a STAR structure check.

The scoring flow

From answer to action plan

  1. Capture. Your typed answer or transcript is evaluated against the selected question.
  2. Measure. Each dimension is normalized to a 0–100 score, then combined into an overall score.
  3. Prioritize. The lowest-scoring dimension becomes your next improvement focus.
  4. Practice. Gaps, uncovered points, and suggestions turn the score into a concrete next attempt.

Question bank

Three interview modes

Practice technical, HR, and behavioral questions from the curated local question bank and supplemental set. Behavioral responses are additionally checked for Situation, Task, Action, and Result cues.

Privacy by design

Your practice stays local

Session metadata, answers, and scores are stored in the app’s SQLite database. Audio is used temporarily for transcription and removed after processing unless explicitly configured otherwise.
